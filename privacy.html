PenguDoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

About this policy

PenguDoku (the “Game”) is a puzzle game for Android published by Rana Patel (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Game uses and how it is handled. By installing and playing the Game you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not run our own servers that store your data.

The Game saves a small amount of data on your device only, such as your current level, progress, and settings. This data never leaves your device through us, and you can remove it by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the Game.

Advertising

The Game shows rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may automatically collect certain information from your device, for example:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system version, and language
  • Information about how ads are shown and interacted with, and app performance data

Google’s use of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Data described above is shared only with Google AdMob for the purpose of showing and measuring ads, and with Google Play services as needed to run the Game on your device.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help resolve it.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you on our own servers. Game progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Data collected by Google for advertising is controlled by Google, and you can manage it through your Google account and device ad settings.

Security

We keep what the Game stores on your device minimal. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
